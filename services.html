What we do

Services built around
real Orlando HVAC work.

Every service below is work we've actually done, from single-room mini splits to full commercial rooftop units. If your situation doesn't fit neatly into one of these, call us and describe it. Most HVAC problems do.

01

Ductwork Replacement

Old, torn, or poorly insulated ductwork chokes airflow and drives up energy use. We pull it out and replace it with duct that's sized, sealed, and supported correctly.

  • Torn, moldy, or crushed duct removed and replaced
  • Metal and flex duct runs, sized for the space
  • Sealed connections for better airflow and less energy waste
  • Attic and crawlspace work, cleaned up when we're done
Get a Quote
02

Ductless Mini-Split Installation

Additions, garages, and older homes without ductwork are a good fit for ductless. We install single and multi-zone systems, sized for the room they're cooling and heating.

  • Indoor and outdoor unit sized to the space
  • Clean line-set routing and wall penetration work
  • Good fit for additions, garages, and rooms without ducts
  • System charged, tested, and walked through with you
Get a Quote
03

AC System Replacement

When a condenser or air handler is past the point of repair, we handle the full swap. Old equipment out, new equipment set, charged, and tested before we leave.

  • Old equipment removed and hauled away
  • New unit set on a proper pad or stand
  • Refrigerant lines checked, charged, and tested
  • System running and verified before we call it done
Get a Quote
04

Compressor & Component Repair

Not every problem needs a full replacement. We diagnose the actual issue first, whether it's a failed compressor, a bad TXV, a weak capacitor, or a refrigerant leak in the copper lines.

  • Diagnostics to find the real problem, not just symptoms
  • Compressor and TXV replacement
  • Refrigerant leak detection and copper line repair
  • Capacitor, contactor, and electrical component replacement
Get a Quote
05

Coil Cleaning & Preventive Maintenance

A dirty coil makes a system work harder for less cooling. We clean evaporator and condenser coils and check airflow so the system isn't fighting itself.

  • Evaporator and condenser coil cleaning
  • Filter and airflow checks
  • Seasonal inspection before summer or winter loads hit
  • Straight answer on what needs attention now versus later
Get a Quote
06

Commercial & Industrial HVAC

Rooftop package units, commercial ductwork, and ventilation systems for offices, restaurants, and other commercial buildings around Orlando.

  • Rooftop unit installation and service
  • Commercial metal and flex ductwork
  • Ventilation systems for commercial buildings
  • Preventive maintenance for commercial equipment
